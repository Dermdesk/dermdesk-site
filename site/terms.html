2. What DermDesk is

DermDesk is an online platform that connects clients with independent dermal clinicians and therapists (“dermal professionals”) for video consultations, treatment plans, progress tracking and messaging. We provide the technology, bookings and payment processing.

Dermal professionals are not employed by DermDesk. Each dermal professional is responsible for the advice, treatment plans and care they provide. We review applications and the qualifications and documents dermal professionals give us, but we do not provide skin or medical advice ourselves.

3. Important health information

DermDesk isn’t an emergency service. If you need urgent medical help, call 000 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Online consultations have limits. Some skin concerns need to be seen in person or by a doctor, and your dermal professional will tell you if they think yours does. DermDesk does not replace your GP or a specialist. If you notice a new or changing mole, a sore that won’t heal or any other concern you’re worried about, see a doctor.

Results vary from person to person, and we can’t guarantee any particular outcome from a consultation or treatment plan.

  • If your dermal professional cancels, or a consultation can’t go ahead because of a problem on our side, you can choose a full refund or a new time at no extra cost.

7. Video consultations

  • Join on time from a private place, using a device with a camera and a reliable internet connection. Natural light helps your dermal professional see your skin clearly.
  • Don’t record a consultation, or take screenshots of it, without the other person’s permission.
  • If you lose connection, rejoin from your account. If the consultation can’t be completed because of a technical problem on our side, contact us and we’ll make it right.
