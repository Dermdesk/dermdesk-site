How joining works

Apply, get approved, set up your practice and start taking bookings.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Create your account, tell us about your practice and upload your documents. It’s two short steps.

  2. 2

    We review your application

    Our team checks your qualifications and documents, then lets you know the outcome by email.

  3. 3

    Set up your practice

    Add your consultation types, prices and availability, and connect Stripe for payouts.

  4. 4

    Start taking bookings

    Once you’re set up, clients can book you, and everything from intake to follow-up is in your dashboard.

Common questions

Have a question about joining? Contact us.

Apply to join

Who can apply?

Qualified dermal clinicians and therapists, whether you work independently or with a clinic. You’ll share your qualifications, registrations and supporting documents when you apply.

What do I need to apply?

Your contact details, qualifications and registration details, the areas you focus on and supporting documents such as your registration certificate. If you work with a clinic, you can add it too.

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews your application and documents, then lets you know the outcome by email. If we need more information, we’ll get in touch.

Do I set my own prices and hours?

Yes. You choose your consultation types, durations, prices and availability.

How do clients find me?

When clients book, they choose a dermal professional based on focus areas and experience. Your profile shows your qualifications, focus areas and consultation types.

How much is DermDesk for professionals?

DermDesk currently has two subscription options: $59 a month for individual dermal professionals, and $249 a month for clinics, with up to 5 professional seats on one bill.

How do I get paid?

Clients pay when they book. Stripe processes payments and sends your earnings to your bank account.

Do I need my own video software?

No. Video consults are built in, and you and your client both join from your DermDesk accounts.

Can I join with my clinic?

Yes. Add your clinic or business when you apply. Independent practitioners are welcome too.

How is client information kept private?

Client photos and consultation notes are private to the client and their dermal professional. Your own documents are encrypted and only seen by our verification team.
