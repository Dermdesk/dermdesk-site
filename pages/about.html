About DermDesk

Skin care advice that fits around your life

DermDesk connects people across Australia with qualified dermal professionals over video, then keeps the support going after the consult is over.

Why we built DermDesk

Getting good advice about your skin often means waiting weeks for an appointment, travelling to a clinic and leaving with a list of products you’re not sure how to use. When something changes, you start again.

We think it should be simpler. With DermDesk you see a dermal professional from home, get a step-by-step treatment plan made for your skin, and stay in touch as you go. Your photos, plan and messages live in one place, so you always know what to do next.

For dermal professionals, DermDesk takes care of the bookings, payments and admin around a consult, so they can spend their time on what they do best: helping clients with their skin.

What we stand for

Qualified, reviewed professionals

Every dermal professional applies to join and our team reviews their qualifications and documents before they can take bookings.

Care that carries on

A consult is the start, not the end. Treatment plans, progress photos and messages keep you supported between visits.

Private by design

Your photos and consultation notes are private to you and your dermal professional. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

Clear, upfront pricing

No subscription and no lock-in. You see the price before you book and only pay for the consults you choose.
