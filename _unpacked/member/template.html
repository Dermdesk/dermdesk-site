Skin concerns we help with

Clinicians on DermDesk work with everyday skin concerns, and can build a routine around the products you already use.

  • Acne & breakouts

    Ongoing breakouts, congestion and blackheads.

  • Hormonal skin

    Flare-ups around the chin and jaw that come and go with your cycle.

  • Pigmentation

    Sun spots, melasma and uneven skin tone.

  • Rosacea & sensitivity

    Redness, flushing and skin that reacts easily.

  • Eczema

    Dry, itchy patches and a skin barrier that needs support.

  • Scarring

    Acne scars, marks and uneven texture.

  • Anti-ageing

    Fine lines, dullness and loss of firmness.

  • Body skin

    Breakouts, dryness and rough texture on the back, chest and arms.

Not sure what’s behind your skin concern? Start with an initial consultation and work it out together.

Who are the clinicians?

Qualified skin clinicians who have applied to DermDesk and been reviewed by our team. Each profile shows their focus areas and experience, so you can choose who you see.

Can I choose my clinician?

Yes. When you book, you can see each clinician’s focus areas and experience, and choose who you’d like to see.

What can you help with online?

Common concerns like acne, pigmentation, rosacea, eczema and scarring, as well as building a routine that suits your skin. If something needs to be seen in person, your clinician will let you know.

How much does a consult cost?

An initial consultation is $149, a follow-up is $99 and a quick review is $59. You’ll see the price before you book, and you pay securely when you book.

How long is a consult?

Initial consultations are 45 minutes, follow-ups are 30 minutes and quick reviews are 15 minutes.

What do I need for a video consult?

A phone or computer with a camera and a good internet connection. Natural light helps your clinician see your skin clearly. You join from your DermDesk account.

What happens after my consult?

Your clinician shares your treatment plan in your account. You can upload progress photos, message your clinician and book a follow-up when you’re ready.

Can I message my clinician between consults?

Yes. You can message your clinician from your account and share photos or PDFs.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your photos and consultation notes are always private to you and your clinician, and payments are processed by Stripe.
